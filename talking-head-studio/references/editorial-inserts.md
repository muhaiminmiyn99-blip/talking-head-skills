# Editorial inserts and A-roll enhancement devices

Direct review of A1-A5 (2026-10-21) showed that the "My work"/AFTER panels are not caption-only. They also contain short full-frame graphic inserts (A3, A4) and enhancement devices layered on A-roll (A1, A2, A5). This file records them so they can be used deliberately. They are a **secondary** grammar: the default B-roll remains the black-and-gold system in [broll-higgsfield.md](broll-higgsfield.md). Use an editorial insert only when the request, the selected preset family or the narration calls for it, and never mix it with black-and-gold B-roll inside one beat.

Evidence: [A3 timeline](visuals/inserts-timeline-A3.jpg), [A4 timeline](visuals/inserts-timeline-A4.jpg) (2 fps, cropped to the My work panel), [A1](visuals/aroll-timeline-A1.jpg), [A2](visuals/aroll-timeline-A2.jpg), [A5](visuals/aroll-timeline-A5.jpg) (2 fps, AFTER panel) and [frame-by-frame transitions](visuals/aroll-transition-frames.jpg). Sheet timestamps are approximate sampling positions.

| A3 11.0-14.5 s | Speaker shrinks into a picture-in-picture surrounded by other clips while the behind-subject word persists; an orange brush-stroke wipe | Speaker-in-grid for "everyone does this"; organic brush wipe as transition. |

| A4 2.1-3.4 s | Off-white paper with grid lines, speaker in a circular cut-out, red serif italic keyword ("Cheating") | Speaker cut-out on light editorial page; serif italic accent word. |
| A4 5.8-9.3 s | "10 simple principles" headline with marker underline, two rows of numbered blue circles, then fan-out beams to platform icons | Numbered system / framework visual. |
| A4 10.3-11.5 s | Photo with "+ Name" pill label and small icons | Guest/person tag. |
| A4 11.5-15 s | Navy collage of blurred screenshots/cards, white line text with blue highlight span ("100k followers", "10 Million") and a swoosh underline | Achievement/scale beat on dark collage. |
| A4 15-17.3 s | Speaker picture-in-picture over a dim wall of thumbnails; blue highlight bar caption | Speaker over social proof wall. |
| A4 17.3-19.3 s | Stack of retro CRT TVs with coloured faces, condensed serif italic headline ("200 Million") with small pill tag | Retro object metaphor for audience/reach. |

Shared traits: off-white paper or near-black plates; one accent colour per video (A3 orange, A4 blue + occasional red/magenta); sans + italic-serif pairing; tiny corner micro-text and rule lines as texture; inserts last ~1-4 s and cut back to the speaker on the next spoken phrase.

## A-roll enhancement devices (observed)

| Device | Where | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Face-tracking HUD | A1 ~2-3 s | Dashed yellow brackets/box tracking the eyes during the hook question. Needs real face tracking; skip if tracking is unstable. |
| Stacked keyword list | A1 ~9.5-11.5 s | Small lead-in with yellow dot markers, then keywords added one per line (Filters / Fluids / Battery), centred. |
| Dashed connector line | A1 ~5-6.5 s | Vertical dashed yellow line linking the lead-in to the boxed keyword below. |
| Neon line frames | A2 throughout | Red glowing tube lines forming corner brackets and stepped angular rails around caption blocks; lines draw on. |
| Small 3D/glossy icons | A1 phone & location pin, A2 coin/tag icons, A5 people & alarm-clock icons | Placed beside or above the keyword, same accent colour; one icon per caption maximum. |
| Particle/object overlay | A5 ~5.5-6.5 s | Falling 3D banknotes over the speaker on "10% Back". Use only for a literal money/benefit beat. |
| Product cutaway with captions | A5 ~13-14.5 s | Close-up product shot while narration and captions continue (pill captions). |

## Transitions (measured from 30 fps frame strips, provisional)

- **Whip / rotational motion-blur cut:** ~8-12 frames (≈270-400 ms) of heavy directional blur between shots; captions are carried into the blur rather than removed first (A1 ~12 s, A5 ~6.8 s and ~11.3 s, A2 ~2 s).
- **Colour-tint flash:** a saturated tint (green in A1 ~11 s) ramps in over ~6 frames (≈200 ms) before the cut.
- **White flash:** 1-3 frames of near-white at a cut (A5 ~3.5 s, A1 end of signage pan).
- **Word wipe-in:** additional keyword appears with a left-to-right reveal over ~3-4 frames (≈100-130 ms) (A1 "ESSENTIALS").
- **Word wipe-out:** caption erased left-to-right over ~3 frames as the shot whips away (A1 "Right Here").
- **Signage push/tilt:** fast camera push or tilt onto the business sign as an establishing beat (A1, A2 endings). Only applicable when the user's footage contains such a shot.

These are measurements on low-resolution portfolio exports, so treat frame counts as ±2 frames. Hard cuts are infrequent; scene detection at threshold 0.3 found only 4-6 hard cuts per A-roll reference because most changes are blur/flash transitions.

## Rules for using them

- Choose devices for meaning: a list device only for an actual list, a counter only for a real number the user supplied, a money overlay only for a money beat.
- Maximum one enhancement device per caption and roughly one notable transition per 3-5 s of speech; leave plain phrase captions in between.
- Never fabricate stats, follower counts, guest names, social-proof walls or screenshots. Placeholders must be marked and replaced with user material before delivery.
- Do not reuse the reference creators' footage, photos, faces, brands (SPH, Cafe Daily, social profiles) or the portfolio frame.
- Editorial inserts are built deterministically (layout, type, counters) with optional generated imagery for textures/objects; generated text is never trusted.
